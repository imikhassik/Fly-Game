Fly Game ideas

Goal of the game
The goal is to make as many babies as possible during the fly lifetime with increasingly better stats. The points are the count of babies the family line has made so far. 
To make babies a fly lands on a platform with a female fly and impgregnates her. It takes time for her to give birth and maybe not all of them will give birth. The birth is celebrated and the counter increases by the amount of babies that were made. The mom sends you a picture of one of her offspring (they are all the same), which is going to inherit some positive traits you have developed so far and some negative traits possibly inherited from the mother. The appearance also is some of your traits and some of mothers traits. This is a descendant candidate.
 
When the main fly dies, the best of its offspring will continue the family line and increasing of the counter. 
If the fly is killed before it has made any babies, the family will die.
The life of one fly is 30 minutes. Its states are improving gradually and it makes better and better babies.

The game can go on forever and the users will have a leaderboard with current fly they have and the number of descendants along with the linear history of the family (fly pictures and how many babies each couple made). The flies will start out all the same but will end up pretty unique in stats and appearance. Possibly we'll have a server to pvp the flies.
The descendant flies can be named, but every one starts out with the first fly named Вова (Boba).
Sometime the platform will contain a male fly and if my fly tries to mate with it, it will get hurt. Some female flies will not want to mate, so my fly can give it something, like portion of what they ate (feed it from mouth), but it's not guaranteed that she doesn't fly away or hurt him.
