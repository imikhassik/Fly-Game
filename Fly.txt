Fly Game ideas

Goal of the game
The goal is to make as many babies as possible during the fly lifetime with increasingly better stats. The points are the count of babies the family line has made so far. 
To make babies a fly lands on a platform with a female fly and impgregnates her. It takes time for her to give birth and maybe not all of them will give birth. The birth is celebrated and the counter increases by the amount of babies that were made. The mom sends you a picture of one of her offspring (they are all the same), which is going to inherit some positive traits you have developed so far and some negative traits possibly inherited from the mother. The appearance also is some of your traits and some of mothers traits. This is a descendant candidate.
 
When the main fly dies, the best of its offspring will continue the family line and increasing of the counter. 
If the fly is killed before it has made any babies, the family will die.
The life of one fly is 30 minutes. Its stats are improving gradually and it makes better and better babies.

The game can go on forever and the users will have a leaderboard with current fly they have and the number of descendants along with the linear history of the family (fly pictures and how many babies each couple made). The flies will start out all the same but will end up pretty unique in stats and appearance. Possibly we'll have a server to pvp the flies.
The descendant flies can be named, but every one starts out with the first fly named Вова (Boba).
Sometime the platform will contain a male fly and if my fly tries to mate with it, it will get hurt. Some female flies will not want to mate, so my fly can give it something, like portion of what they ate (feed it from mouth), but it's not guaranteed that she doesn't fly away or hurt him.

Let's break this down into a clear ordered feature list with dependencies. Each step builds on the previous, so we'll start with the foundation and add layers.



Ordered Feature List
1. Stat System Core
What: Define and store fly stats (spd, rof, max_hp, cap_hp), generation scores (gen_spd, gen_rof, gen_hp), and the logic to update them from food buffs.
Why: Everything else depends on these numbers. The game must track current stats, max caps, and accumulated gen scores.

2. Baby Inheritance Logic
What: Implement the baby stat formula, birth chance decay (after 3 babies), and the "best baby" selection when the fly dies (based on sum of stats).
Why: This is the core of the lineage system. It requires the stat system (step 1) to be complete.

3. Female Fly Stats
What: Each female fly gets randomized stats (e.g., between 30%–80% of the player's current stats, with some variation). Add a UI tooltip or popup that shows her stats when the player is near (e.g., press a key to inspect).
Why: The player needs to make an informed decision. This is a new UI layer but depends on stat definitions.

4. Mating with Female Stats
What: When mating, the baby's stats are influenced by the female's stats. Example formula:
baby_stat = (player_stat * 0.7 + female_stat * 0.3) * (0.8–1.2) + gen_bonus
This gives the female a ~30% influence. If the female has poor stats, it hurts the baby. If she has good stats, it helps.
Why: This adds strategy and makes the female stats meaningful.

5. Random Collectibles ("Sparkles")
What: Small floating objects (e.g., glowing motes) that spawn randomly on platforms or in the air. Collecting one gives a tiny permanent boost:

50% chance to add 0.001 to a random main stat (spd, rof, max_hp, cap_hp)

50% chance to add 0.001 to a random generation score (gen_spd, gen_rof, gen_hp)
They despawn after a few seconds unless collected.
Why: Fills the "empty" feeling while flying. Gives a reason to fly around even when not fighting.

6. Enemy Targeting of Collectibles
What: When the fly is landed (and not in danger), enemies will ignore the fly and instead chase the nearest collectible. When an enemy reaches a collectible, it destroys it (the player loses the chance).
Why: This makes the landing phase more interesting and gives the player a reason to stay active (or risk losing boosts).

7. Baby Born Popup
What: A simple UI notification that says "Baby Born!" with the baby's stats (or summary). Could be a toast message.
Why: Already agreed; it's a small UI addition that depends on the baby logic.

8. Lineage Database (SQLite)
What: Create the SQLite tables (runs, babies, lineage) and implement saving/loading of stats, family tree, and run history.
Why: This is the persistent storage layer. It should be built after the stat and baby logic are stable so we know exactly what to save.

9. Game Over / Continue Flow
What: The screens and logic for "Game Over" (no babies) vs "Continue with best baby" (if babies exist). Includes displaying the best baby's stats and a "Continue" button.
Why: This is the end‑of‑run experience. It ties together the baby selection and lineage storage.

10. UI Polish
What: Display current stats, generation scores, baby count, and the 30‑minute timer on the HUD. Possibly a lineage tree viewer (simple list).
Why: This wraps everything into a playable experience. It can be done incrementally but should be near the end to avoid rework.
